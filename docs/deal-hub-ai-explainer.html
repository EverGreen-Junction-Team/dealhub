Put simply: the rules stop it lying. The Control centre stops it sounding generic. That's why the rules aren't editable — not to withhold control, but because those guarantees are the reason the output can be trusted at all.

The safeguard worth understanding

The failure that would kill this product isn't a missed detail. It's one confident, invented claim — a rep reads "you promised them a discount", finds no such promise, and never trusts the panel again.

So the rule isn't a polite request in the instructions. It's enforced by the software, after the AI answers and before anyone sees it: every claim must carry your customer's exact words, and we check them character by character against the real message.

Message "Keep the whole glazing package under £30k including the garden room."
Claim A "under £30k including the garden room"
Shown — those words are really in the message.
Claim B "they want to stay around thirty thousand"
Discarded — a fair summary, but not what they wrote. It never reaches the screen.

Claim B is the important one

It isn't even wrong. We still throw it away. An answer nobody can check is indistinguishable from one that's invented — and we would rather show your reps less than have them doubt any of it.

Two more things follow from the same principle. Every answer states what it read — "Read 5 of 11 messages · 2 calls not transcribed" — so a thin answer explains itself instead of looking like a thin deal. And every claim links back to the message it came from, so a rep can check it in one click.

Anyone can buy the same AI. What makes it sound like your business rather than a generic sales tool is the Control centre — and it's empty today.

What we need from you

The Control centre is live in the app now, with four sections. Filled in, it's the difference between an assistant that understands glazing and one that guesses. Left empty, the AI still works — it just answers like it has never seen a glazing quote before.

None of it needs to be perfect. Rough and specific beats polished and vague, and it can be edited any time.
