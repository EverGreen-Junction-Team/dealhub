Deal Hub new add-on · decision brief

Operations Calendar

Deal Hub shows deals as a list. This add-on shows them as a diary — every booked appointment laid out by day and by person, with the gaps visible.

Date17 Nov 2026 Decision neededWhich of the two below Read time2 minutes

How it works

Appointments come from HighLevel, where reps' Google and Outlook calendars already sync both ways. Deal Hub reads them, shows them as a diary, and sends changes back — so there is one copy of the truth, not two. Every appointment arrives carrying its customer, its deal and its value, because Deal Hub already holds all of that.

The choice

Option A

Shared week

  • One calendar holding everyone's appointments, filtered by person when you want
  • Drag to reschedule, plus book, reassign and cancel
  • Click an appointment to open its deal
  • Answers what is booked
CostSmaller build. No licence.
Option B

Board, a column per person

  • A column for each surveyor or fitter, read down the day
  • Everything in option A, plus moving work between people at a glance
  • Click an appointment to open its deal
  • Answers who should take it
CostNotably bigger build, and a commercial calendar component — an ongoing licence, not a one-off.

Option A is a way of seeing the work. Option B is a way of allocating it. If the calendar is meant to be used for deciding who does what, it is B, and that is worth knowing before anything is quoted.

What's needed to start

  1. A or B. Everything else follows from this, including the price.
  2. Which sub-account to pilot on. Then the order the rest follow in.
  3. Whether blocked-out time has to appear. If reps block travel or admin in their diary and it must show on the board, that changes the groundwork.
  4. Whether moving an appointment should email the customer. HighLevel can do it automatically. Easy to switch on, awkward to undo once customers have had the emails.

Not affected

Already live, unchanged by this

Deals · customers · stage history · messages · tasks · notes · users. All of it keeps running exactly as it does today. The appointment is the only thing the system doesn't already hold.
